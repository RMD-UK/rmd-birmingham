What this does

Creates a practice course called TEST Course with 15 made-up candidates, plus an earlier course (TEST Earlier) holding the previous attempts of two repeat candidates. Use it to rehearse registers, assessment allocation, the assessment form, the retest pool and the repeat-instructor warning before the real assessment night. Remove it when you have finished.

  • All test emails end in .test@rmd-test.invalid. They cannot reach a real inbox, and the site does not copy them to the Master Google Sheet.
  • Both test courses are set to capacity 0, so the global allocation never places real candidates on them.
  • Nothing here touches real candidates, courses or sign-ins.
