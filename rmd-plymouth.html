About

RMD at the University of Plymouth

RMD Plymouth is a newer RMD UK site, delivering near-peer Basic Life Support training to healthcare students at the University of Plymouth. Like every RMD site, it trains and develops its own student instructors, and is part of the RMD UK network alongside Birmingham, Bristol, Warwick and Brunel.

The Programme

The near-peer pathway at Plymouth

RMD Plymouth follows the same development pathway used across the RMD UK network.

1

Candidate

Receives BLS training from near-peer instructors.

2

Instructor

Trains to teach BLS to the next cohort of candidates.

3

Assessor

Quality-assures teaching and signs off competency.

4

Senior Faculty

Leads chapter governance and faculty development.
