Find a candidate - first sit or not

Search any candidate by name, email or degree (across every course, or narrow it with the optional course box); the result shows their course with its date, room and assessment slot. Not first sit takes them out of the standard first-sit assessment stream, so they are not given a room or slot automatically and RMD faculty arrange their assessment separately. Cannot attend is for someone who will not be at the assessment (for example, absent from the course); it also frees any slot they already hold. Both can be reversed here at any time.

Allocate assessment slots

Allocates every eligible candidate on the chosen course who doesn't already have an assessment slot — one room, one 15-minute slot each, filled earliest-slot-first across every room so the whole group finishes as early as possible. Rooms are shuffled unbiased (Fisher-Yates), same as room allocation elsewhere on this site. Already-slotted candidates are left untouched; re-running only fills in anyone new.
