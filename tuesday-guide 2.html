Tuesday Night Hub: user guide

Running the Tuesday evening BLS refresher for nursing students

1. What it is

One place to run the Tuesday refresher: who is in which room, the register, assessment results, and the nursing school sheet. Sign in with your RMD account. It works on a phone, laptop and big screen.

Open the Tuesday Night HubRMD resource hub

Who can use it: RMD faculty (Director, Student Faculty, Senior Faculty), assessors and Senior Instructors. Instructors see a short list. RMD faculty see everything. RMD faculty do not assess on Tuesday.

2. Before the term RMD faculty, Planning

  • Which Tuesdays run: set the first and last date, then confirm each evening. 15 Dec to 5 Jan is off by default.
  • Add nurses and rooms: paste one nurse per line: date, room, last name, first name, email, course, year. Press Parse & preview, check it, then Add. Maximum 8 per room. Leave the room empty for a random room.
  • Check the list: paste your list again and press Check against what is in the hub to find anyone extra, missing or in the wrong room. Remove duplicates on every evening clears double entries.
  • Teaching shifts: shows the RMD faculty lead and the teacher in each room every night. See section 5.
  • Test evening: a practice night with made-up nurses. Nothing goes to the nursing school.

3. On the night: instructors Assess a room

  • Pick your room. You see your nurses as cards.
  • Mark each nurse Here, Late or Absent. Anyone not marked by 19:30 shows as Late automatically.
  • For each nurse who attended, tap Pass or Fail for BLS and AED, and for first aid. Passing means passing both parts.
  • On a Fail, tap what went wrong (choose Other to type your own), then Save result.
  • Use Concern if you are worried about a nurse. It goes to RMD faculty only.
  • At the end of your session, tap Register is complete. This is shown as a to-do until you do it.

4. On the night: RMD faculty Tonight

  • Register: groups for Not marked, Here, Late and Absent, plus which rooms have confirmed.
  • Room lists: every room with its teacher and nurses. Tap a nurse to move them to another room (not above 8), fix their details, or remove them from the evening.
  • Needs another date: move an absent nurse to a later evening. A nurse can be moved up to 3 times, and the 3rd asks you to confirm because it is more than usual.
  • Results: pass tally and who needs a private word.
  • Concerns: close each one with an outcome and a note.
  • Live timetable and End of night (final checks and a results backup).
  • Big screens (Screens and help): Register and Results, numbers only. Press Full screen. Exit with Esc, the Exit button or a double click.

5. Teaching shifts everyone who teaches

  • Every room has one teacher every night, and the RMD faculty lead for the night is shown at the top.
  • If you cannot make a night: open Teaching shifts, tap your name, then Can't make it. Swap with a teacher on another night, or ask someone free that night to take it.
  • Nothing changes until the other teacher accepts. You stay on the rota until then.
  • Swaps close 24 hours before the night. After that, ask RMD faculty. The hub will not allow a change that leaves a room empty.

6. After the night RMD faculty, Planning

  • Not passed follow-up: rare. Prepare the message to the nursing school, send it by email, then tap I have sent it. The school deals with the student case by case. Students absent on the last evening are handled the same way.
  • Nursing school sheet: download once every room is confirmed, everyone is marked and everyone who attended has a result. Columns: name, email, year, arrived, result (Passed or Not passed). Anyone who did not attend is listed underneath.
  • Past evenings: on the Register of a past evening, mark everyone Here and passed fills it in. Absent stays absent.

7. Rules to remember

  • Maximum 8 nurses per room.
  • Late is automatic from 19:30. Late on the school sheet follows the register mark.
  • Pass means both BLS and AED, and first aid.
  • Names are never shown on the big screens.
  • Test evening results never reach the nursing school.
