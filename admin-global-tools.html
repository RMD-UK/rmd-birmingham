Certificates
CPD Certificate — master on/off

This is the one place the "Download your CPD certificate" link on everyone's My Account page is switched on or off. Set the academic year it's for, then tick Open once you're confident Role History is accurate for everyone who delivered that year — closing it again hides the download for everyone until you reopen it. There's no per-person override: if someone left or didn't do the job, delete their account instead, which already removes them from eligibility.
