Logistics
📍 At a glance
📅
Dates
Three evening sessions: two teaching sessions, then your assessment night. Your own dates appear when you look up your course below. Assessment night instructions are sent in the week before.
🏛
Venue
All teaching is in the evening and held on-campus, in the Medical School building, University of Birmingham
Edgbaston, Birmingham, B15 2TT.
⏰
Start time
Please arrive ready to start at 18:15 in the ATH (lecture theatre on the top floor) to receive your introductory lecture on session 1 and 2. You will then go on for small group teaching. The session will end at 21:00.
📄
Before you come
The BLS reference material below has the algorithm and AED guidance. Please take a look before your course starts.
🚪
Your course, room and assessment
Your room is confirmed before you attend, and definitely by the time the course starts on the first Monday. Your assessment slot is shown once it has been allocated. Look up your course, room and assessment below.
🅿️
Parking
See the University of Birmingham car parking information on the main University website.
👕
Dress
Smart casual is not needed, but wear something comfortable. You will be kneeling and doing practical manoeuvres on the floor.
💇
Hair and make-up
Please tie your hair back. Please do not wear excessive make-up, lipstick especially, as it marks our manikins.
✉️
Can't make a session?
Email colmds-c-rmdbirmingham@adf.bham.ac.uk with as much notice as you can. It really helps our admin team.
Find your course, room and assessment

Enter your degree, full name and email address exactly as you registered, and we'll tell you which course you are on, which room you are in, and when your assessment is. If nothing shows yet, your allocation hasn't been confirmed - check back nearer the time rather than worrying.
