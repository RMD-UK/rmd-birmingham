Duplicate People & Account Cleanup
RMD Instructor Weekend 2026 · Director only
Scan
⚠️ Removes duplicate records from the people collection — the list attendance.html, room allocation and every other check-in tool reads from. A duplicate here usually means Bulk User Setup created a fresh login for someone who already had a record from the Instructor Weekend registration sync, without checking first.

This tool only ever deletes the older, no-login record — never the record tied to someone's actual Firebase login (that one is always kept). Nothing is deleted until you tick it and confirm.

Built 2026-09-21: deleting a record that does have a login attached (below, in "Remove a specific record", or a ticked "needs review" group) now also deletes that Firebase login, not just the data — so nothing is left behind as an orphaned empty account. Previously the login survived as an unused shell, which is the exact pattern behind this year's duplicate-account mix-ups.
Groups records that share an email address or a name (case/spacing-insensitive). Two records for the same person under a genuinely different name AND email won't be caught here — use "Remove a specific record" below for those.

Remove a specific record
For duplicates the scan above can't group automatically — e.g. the same person under two different email addresses with different names. Search by email or name, review the matches, then delete the one(s) you don't want. Deleting a record with a login attached deletes that login entirely, not just their data — make sure you're deleting the spare, not the one someone actually signs in with. This can't be undone.
