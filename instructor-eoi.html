Instructor Expression of Interest

RMD Birmingham — Resuscitation · Mentorship · Development

This is for people who have attended and passed one of RMD Birmingham's BLS courses and want to be considered for training as an Instructor.

You can submit this at any point during the year. Submitting an expression of interest does not guarantee you a place — places are confirmed at the end of the year, once we've checked who's passed and how they've been assessed during their time on the course.

↓ What being an Instructor involves — please read before submitting ↓

What being an Instructor involves

This isn't the full Memorandum of Understanding, and you're not being asked to sign or agree to anything here — RMD members complete the full MoU separately, only once they've actually been confirmed in a role. It's shown now so you know what's expected before you put your name forward.

Role-specific expectations — Instructor

  • Deliver BLS/AED/First Aid training for 18 timetabled teaching sessions (across all 6 BLS courses) in Semesters 1 and 2.
  • Ensure delivery aligns with the relevant resuscitation council standards and RMD guidance.

Core expectations of all RMD members

  • Punctuality and attendance: be on time for all allocated sessions, required training, and meetings, including the annual instructor weekend.
  • Professionalism: demonstrate the highest level of professionalism, respect, and courtesy towards all peers, faculty, and candidates, and act as a positive ambassador for RMD and the University of Birmingham.
  • Quality and reflection: work cooperatively to deliver high-quality teaching, be approachable and encouraging, and respond to feedback from mentors/faculty.
  • Safeguarding and equality: act in accordance with the University of Birmingham's policies on equality, diversity and safeguarding, including in the handling of any personal or assessment data encountered through RMD activities.
