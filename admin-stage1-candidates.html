Course and room names below are free text for now — the definitive teaching-room list hasn't been confirmed yet, so nothing here is locked to a fixed list. "Promote to Instructor" creates the person a real account (Firebase login + a people record) and starts their role history — do this only for someone actually being made an Instructor, not as a test.
