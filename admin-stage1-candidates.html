Add candidates

Paste one candidate per line: last name, first name, email, degree, student ID, year of study — matches copying columns straight out of a spreadsheet. Only last name and first name are required. Email, degree, student ID and year of study are all optional (leave blank between commas to skip) — most students won't have a student ID on file, so leave that column empty rather than holding a row back. Course is no longer typed in here at all — it's always worked out automatically from the programme's deadline and this year's course dates (set on Stage 1 Settings), shown with an "auto" badge in the preview so you can check it before importing; year of study isn't used anywhere yet but is captured now so it's already on file when it is. Tab or comma separated. Room isn't collected here any more — that's set on Course Room & Instructor Allocation (3.6), once you've confirmed who's actually on the course.

Import assessment outcomes

Paste rows from your outcomes sheet: email, pass/fail, more than 2 attempts. Matched by email against the candidates already imported above. Pass/fail accepts "pass", "fail", "passed", "failed", "exempt" (case doesn't matter) — anything else is flagged so it's not silently guessed. Attempts accepts a number (e.g. 3) or yes/no. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Import placement dates

Paste rows straight from a placement lead's sheet (Pharmacy, or any other programme this comes up for): first name, last name, email, start date, end date — dates as DD/MM/YYYY. Matched to candidates already imported above by email. A student can have several rows (several separate placement blocks); each one is added to their file, so re-pasting the same sheet again won't create duplicates. A course runs from its first teaching Monday through its week-3 assessment evening (worked out from the date set on Stage 1 Settings) — if any of a candidate's placement dates overlap that whole span for their assigned course, they're flagged here and on the Candidates list below so you can move them by hand. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
