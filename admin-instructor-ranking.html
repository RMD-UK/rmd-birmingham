Instructor Ranking
RMD Birmingham · Director & Senior Faculty only · Back to Instructor Potential Review

Ranks everyone who sent an Expression of Interest and passed. The score is the average OPIS total (out of 15), plus a bonus for potential outstanding teaching ability (scaled by how many raters ticked it), plus a bonus for an Excellent pass note, minus a penalty for passing at attempt 3 or 4. Passing at attempt 1 or 2 ranks the same. Enter the number of places to see a selection that keeps the intake in proportion to the degrees we teach, without dropping below the standard you set. Nothing is saved until a Director presses Choose.
