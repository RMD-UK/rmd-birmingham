Roles and access

Who has director access, and the dated history of every person's role.

About director access

This is the year-round place to grant or remove director-level platform access (see all data, manage the platform). It is separate from the Course Director tag used for Instructor Weekend: someone can have one without the other.

It is also separate from Faculty Responses, Directors and roles, which only lists people who sent this year's weekend RSVP. The lists below are the full annual roster.

About role history

Every promotion, stand-down or renewal is recorded as a dated period on the person's own record, instead of overwriting one "current role" field. The eligibility shortlist, CPD certificate and annual review read from this.

Adding a period never deletes or edits an earlier one: it closes whatever period was open and starts a new one. Nothing here is automatic, so use the buttons when a role actually changes.

The CPD certificate year and open/closed switch is in Global tools, Switches and dates.

One-time backfill (rarely needed)
Use "Set roles from the MOU roster" instead. This backfill copies each person's people.role, which also holds their Instructor Weekend job (such as faculty or itc), and the Standing display ignores most of those values. Only use it if you understand that.

People who have a current role but no role history can be given one open period starting from their current role. The start date is not known, so it is recorded as "from: unknown" rather than guessed.

Set permanent roles from the MOU roster

Sets each person's permanent role (Instructor, Senior Instructor or Assessor) from this year's MOU roster, using the exact spellings the Standing display reads. Student and Senior Faculty are skipped because their standing comes from the faculty roster. Nothing is written until you review the list and confirm. Earlier role periods are closed, never deleted.

People registered as Instructor Candidate for this Instructor Weekend are left out and listed separately, because they only become Instructors through Pass Confirmation.
