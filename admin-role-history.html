Role History
RMD Birmingham · Director only
Every promotion, stand-down, or renewal gets recorded as a dated period against the person's own record, instead of just overwriting a single "current role" field. This is Section 0.3 of the platform workplan — the eligibility shortlist, CPD certificate and annual review are all meant to read from this once they're built, so this page exists to make the data real and checkable before those depend on it.

Adding a role period never deletes or edits an earlier one — it closes whatever period was open and starts a new one. Nothing here is automatic yet: there's no annual review screen calling this, so use the actions below directly when a role actually changes.

The CPD certificate's year + open/closed master switch has moved to Global Admin Tools — this page only feeds the role-history data it reads from.
Ratify permanent roles from the MoU roster

Sets each person's permanent role (Instructor, Senior Instructor or Assessor) from this year's MoU roster, using the exact spellings the Standing display reads. Student and Senior Faculty are skipped, because their standing comes from the faculty roster. Nothing is written until you review the list and confirm. Earlier role periods are closed, never deleted.

People registered as Instructor Candidate for this Instructor Weekend are left out and listed separately, because they only become Instructors through Pass Confirmation.
One-time backfill
Use "Ratify permanent roles" above instead. This backfill copies each person's people.role, which also holds their Instructor Weekend job (such as faculty or itc), and the Standing display ignores most of those values. Only use it if you understand that.

People who already have a current role but no role history yet (i.e. everyone, before this page existed) can be seeded with a single open period starting from their current role, so the eligibility shortlist and CPD certificate have something to read immediately rather than waiting a full year. The exact start date isn't known for a backfilled period, so it's recorded as "from: unknown" rather than guessed.
