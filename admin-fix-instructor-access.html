What this fixes

Some instructors were added to the website before they had a login, so their record sits under a placeholder ID. When they sign in, the site cannot find that record, so they get no Current Course or OPIS links and are not matched to their room.

This tool moves everything for each affected person (role, room teams, OPIS access, Instructor Weekend attendance and feedback) onto their real login. People who have no login yet are listed and left alone. It is safe to run again.
