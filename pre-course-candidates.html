RMD Birmingham · Instructor Weekend 2026

Your candidate guide

Everything you need before and during the weekend — dates, what to expect, and how to find your way around the course platform — in one place.

The basics

Saturday 10 OctRegistration from 0800h · course runs to approximately 1730h Sunday 11 OctRegistration from 0930h · course runs to approximately 1630h VenueBirmingham Medical School, University of Birmingham, B15 2TT

The weekend has two parts: a BLS Provider refresher, where you demonstrate your own resuscitation skills, followed by a BLS Instructor course, where you learn and practise how to teach them. After the weekend, you'll need to instruct on at least two further courses before you're officially recognised as a BLS Instructor.

Before you arrive

Pre-course reading

Before the weekend, you need to know the basic life support and first aid algorithms, and be familiar with the instructor course details — covered in the BLS skills manual and Instructor Course manual. Some manual pages are deliberately redacted; that's outdated content due for a future update, and we'll teach you the current material on the course.

All of this, along with the QCPR app you'll need to access, is on the Resources hub — go there now and make sure you're familiar with everything before you arrive.

Signing in

Your account & timetable

Use your existing RMD account

You already have an RMD account — sign in at rmd.uk.com with the same email and password you've used before. From the Member Menu, tap RMD Instructor Weekend to reach your timetable.

The timetable is live — a highlighted banner always shows whichever session is running now, and once room groups are set, the same page shows a banner across the top with your room for the weekend. Bookmark it to your phone's home screen before Saturday if you'd like to — we'll make sure the day runs to schedule and keep you posted as we go, so the timetable is there for your own awareness rather than something you'll miss out on by not watching it.

On arrival

Checking in on Saturday morning

Registration desk

On arrival, head to the registration desk, run by the RMD faculty team. They'll help you check in using the poster QR code — scan it, type your name to find yourself on the list, and confirm you've arrived. This is also where you'll be issued your RMD polo shirt on the first day.

Left: the poster you'll see on arrival. Right: what scanning it opens.
Through the weekend

Your room & the Noticeboard

The blue banner at the top of your timetable shows your allocated room for the whole weekend. Below it, the Noticeboard carries any messages from the Course Director — tap ✓ Dismiss to clear one you've read.

Room Rosters

Handy if you get lost

Tap 🏠 Rooms at the top of the timetable to see every room's Instructor Trainers and candidates side by side — useful for finding a friend in another group, or if you're not sure which room is which.

Session timers

You may see these running

Your Instructor Trainer may start a countdown during small-group or presentation practice — it's just to keep things on schedule, nothing for you to operate.

Practical details

Meals, social & what to bring

  • Lunch is provided both days, and pizza is provided on Saturday evening.
  • Please bring your own water bottle and use the water fountains.
  • Saturday evening's social is at The Country Girl — a quiz with one free drink included, open to everyone. You'll need to wear your polo shirt. Exact timings to be confirmed.
  • You'll be added to a WhatsApp group for the weekend closer to the course — we'll share further practical details there as they come up.
  • Room group allocations will be sent to you before the course; small-group sessions run in the CM rooms.

Questions any time — before or during the weekend? Email j.hulme.1@bham.ac.uk.
