Assessment Evening Roles

Instructor Guide 2026/27 - part 2 of 6. On assessment evening you'll be allocated a role. Roles 1 to 5 are the instructor roles. Role 6 (Assessor) and role 7 (RMD faculty) are for the assessment team and faculty, and are here so everyone can see how the whole night fits together.

How a candidate moves through the night

Everyone uses the Assessment night door of the Phase 1 hub on their phone, so the screens agree. Each candidate is always in one of five states, and each attempt has its own state.

  • Not hereOn tonight's list, not checked in yet.
  • ArrivedChecked in at the check-in station (Student ID checked) and waiting for their runner.
  • CollectedTheir runner has met them and is taking them to the room. An assessor can also do this with Collect now.
  • Being assessedAn assessor has taken them on. Nobody else can pick them.
  • AssessedThe assessor has saved the result.

A candidate who is referred at attempt 1 starts attempt 2 back at Not here. RMD faculty check them in again when they return from resit teaching (see role 7).

1Welcomer

  • Meet in the foyer of the med school
  • Escort students from the entrance to the pre-exam teaching room
  • This is usually WF15, but double check each exam night
  • Ask the student what time their exam is
  • If they've come super early, tell them to come back closer to their exam time - 15-20 minutes before

2Pre-Exam Teaching

  • Usually in the Wolfson Common Room, but double check each exam night
  • Bring a manikin, AED and wipes
  • Students will arrive 15 minutes before their exam
  • Help any students with questions
  • Run through both algorithms as if it were their exam
  • It gets very busy - try to help as many students as possible but time is limited

3Runner

  • You'll have 1 or 2 allocated rooms
  • Come to pre-exam teaching (usually the Wolfson Common Room) to collect your student(s). Call out your room number and the name of the next expected candidate on your list - you'll also have a sign
  • Escort the student from pre-exam teaching to their exam
  • Where the candidate goes next depends on their result - the assessor will tell you which pathway:
    • Passed: they're free to go home
    • Referred at a first sit: the assessor tells you what they failed and why. Escort them to resit teaching and tell the resit instructor what they need to resit and why
    • Not passed at a second sit or later: they are not retested tonight. Take them to RMD faculty, who explain the next steps
  • Come back to pre-exam teaching to collect the next student
On your phone
  1. Open the Phase 1 hub, choose Assessment night, then Runner / instructor. Tap your room to open it.
  2. Each row shows the slot time, the name, the attempt number (1 to 4) and the state. Only people showing Arrived have checked in and can be collected.
  3. Call out your room number and the name of the next expected candidate (the next unassessed name in slot order). When they come to you, tap Collect, then walk them to the room.
  4. Called but nobody comes? Tap Not answering. It counts how many times you've called, and flags them as not there for faculty. If they turn up, check-in or Clear removes the flag.
  5. Collected the wrong person? Tap Put back while they still show Collected.
  6. Once the assessor takes them on, you don't tap anything. The row moves to Assessed when the result is saved.

4Actor / Resit Actor

  • You'll have an allocated room
  • At the start of the night, grab a manikin, AED and wipes
  • Check the AED is working and has sticky pads
  • You'll sit in on the exams
  • In the help scenario, you are the help - double check this with the assessor
  • Pass the AED when prompted by the assessor
  • You'll also be placed in the recovery position for the second half of the exam

5Resit Instructor

  • Double check each exam night where this will be taking place
  • Bring a manikin, AED and wipes
  • The runner will tell you which section the student failed and why
  • Go through that 1-on-1 with the student
  • If they only failed BLS/AED, they only resit BLS/AED. If they only failed recovery position, they only resit recovery position
  • When the student feels ready, escort them to the atrium
  • Student Faculty will allocate the student to a resit room
  • Warn the student there may be a bit of a wait - resit rooms often have queues
  • Tell the student that RMD faculty will check them in again for their second sit

6Assessor

Who assesses whom

  • First sits: assessed by the assessor group.
  • Second sits: need a different assessor from the first sit, who is RMD Senior Faculty, a selected senior member of RMD Student Faculty, or a selected senior external assessor.
  • Third sits and later: Senior Faculty or a named assessor.
  • The hub tells you if you can't take someone on.
  1. Open the Phase 1 hub, choose Assessment night, then Assessor (first sit) or Assessor (second sit). Third sits and later, and RAP candidates, are under Retest-plus & RAP.
  2. Your room's list shows everyone expected, in slot order, with their state. Grey means Not here.
  3. With check-in on, you can only pick someone once they show Collected. If the candidate is Arrived and the runner is slow, tap Collect now and confirm with Yes, collect. It is logged as collected by you.
  4. Tap Start assessment (retests: Take on). The candidate shows Being assessed and nobody else can pick them. If another assessor got there first, you'll see "[name] has taken them on".
  5. Open the form. Mark BLS + AED and First aid (recovery position) separately as Pass or Not yet. For Not yet, tick the steps missed or add a comment. If you mark Pass with a red step, you'll be asked why.
  6. Tap Save result. The candidate shows Assessed. A referral at attempt 1 puts them in the retest pool as attempt 2.
  7. Taken on the wrong person, or the candidate has to leave? Tap Put back before saving.

If check-in is off. A Director decides each evening whether check-in is required. If it is off, you see everyone in your room and can pick anyone, as in earlier years. The Start assessment and Save result steps are the same.

7RMD faculty

For Student Faculty, Senior Faculty and Directors. You run the check-in station, watch the whole night, and look after anyone who is referred.

Check-in station
  1. Open the Phase 1 hub, choose Assessment night, then Check-in station. Where the desk is each night is confirmed at the briefing.
  2. Check the candidate's Student ID first. Then tap their name in the grid. They now show Arrived. A bar offers Undo for a few seconds if you tapped the wrong person.
  3. The grid shows people due in the next hour plus anyone running late, alphabetical by surname. Anyone else: type their name in the search box (early arrivals, wrong slot). The tabs show Not here yet, Arrived, Collected, and Everyone.
  4. Retests and people with no slot sit in their own group at the top. Check them in as normal, then tap Collected when you send them to an assessor.
  5. A red See RMD faculty next to a name means they aren't eligible tonight or have no slot. Sort it out with the Director before checking them in.
  6. Directors: the box at the top of the station has the Check-in required tonight switch. Turn it on once the station is running, so assessors can only pick people who have been collected.
Watching the night
  • Runner view shows every room, with who is arrived, collected, not answering or assessed. Assess now opens the assessor view if you need to step in.
  • Retest pool lists who is waiting for a second sit or later, and Assessment allocation lets you move people between rooms.
  • Flow screen is a big screen for the room. Every candidate is one dot, coloured and numbered by attempt (1 blue, 2 amber, 3 pink, 4 green), moving from Not here to Waiting, Collected, Being assessed and then Passed or referred. It never shows names. A dashed ring means a runner couldn't find them. The panel at the bottom shows counts for RMD faculty. The existing Results screen still shows pass and refer counts by room.
After a referral
  • On the check-in station, the RMD faculty tab lists names: referred tonight (retest to arrange), the other people on the retest list, and anyone not passed at attempt 2, 3 or 4. The Retest pool page has the next steps for each group and a Copy note button. Nothing is emailed from the hub.
  • When a referred candidate returns from resit teaching, check them in again from the same tab. They show on the Flow screen as attempt 2 and wait for a second-sit assessor.
If something goes wrong
  • Runner can't find someone: tap Not answering and tell the check-in station.
  • Checked in the wrong person: tap Undo on the station.
  • Collected the wrong person: tap Put back.
  • Two assessors tap the same person: the first one gets them. The second sees who has them.
  • Someone isn't in the grid: type their name. If they still aren't there, they aren't on tonight's list, so send them to RMD faculty.
  • The phone shows an old state: the screens update by themselves. If one looks stuck, reload the page.
Common fail reasons

Things to keep in mind and look out for in your teaching:

  • Compression rate too fast/slow
  • Unsafe delivery of shock
  • Incorrect positioning of AED pads
  • Touching the patient during rhythm analysis
  • Failing to check breathing after placing the patient in the recovery position

After an exam night, ask your mentor about your room's pass/fail rate and what areas students struggled with - this is also fed back at the next instructor's briefing.
