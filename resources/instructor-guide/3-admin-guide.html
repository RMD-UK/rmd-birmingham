Instructor Guide 2026/27 - part 3 of 6. Feedback forms, pay, equipment, registering and absences.

Feedback forms (student form + STAF)

At the end of Week 2 teaching, two feedback forms need completing:

  • Student Feedback Form - get all your students to fill this out before leaving the last teaching night, usually distributed by QR code. This gauges how students found the teaching.
  • Student-Teacher-Aptitude-Form (STAF) - completed by you, as a room, to suggest any students who'd make good teachers next year. You must submit one for every student in your room after Week 2 of every course.

Both are distributed via the WhatsApp group in Week 2 of teaching.

Worklink / HR / pay

Setting up payment via the University can seem confusing. We must set up payment exactly as the University requires, and most of the process is completely out of our control. To keep things smooth:

  • Complete your right-to-work check on time - you cannot work without this
  • Check your emails regularly (correspondence often comes from WorkHere@contacts.bham.ac.uk)
  • Complete any forms you're asked to as soon as you can

Worklink contact details:

  • Phone: 0121 414 5000
  • Online: universityofbirmingham.service-now.com/worklink

Emails go out on a rolling basis, so your worklink/HR access may not be set up until mid-way through the semester - this is normal and you can back-track your time cards. However, once your assignment is set up, you must submit time cards within two weeks of completing work to be paid. You will not be paid if time cards are submitted late. Details on submitting time cards will come in the relevant emails.

A small handful of people (across all roles) have been incorrectly taxed in the past. This is normally refunded by HMRC by the end of the financial year (refunds usually processed in June), but if you're worried, contact HR or HMRC directly - you should have been given a Tax Guide explaining this.

Any payment setup or receipt issues should go to HR, either via the portal or by phone.

Equipment

Manikins are kept in the manikin cupboard in EF10E, along the anatomy corridor. This should be unlocked before you arrive - message us promptly if you have access issues. Retrieving and returning manikins is your responsibility as an instructor (and as an actor on exam nights).

All other equipment is in the cupboards surrounding the CM rooms.

If you find a fault you can't fix yourself, bring it to Student Faculty (usually in the atrium) and we'll sort it.

Registering

Every week you're expected to sign a paper register to confirm you've worked - this feeds through to HR to confirm payment. The register's location will be confirmed each week at briefing, usually available in the CM atrium during your break. If you forget to sign, contact your mentor as soon as possible so the register can be updated.

Absences

We understand illness and unavoidable commitments happen. If you know you'll be absent, with at least a week's notice (unless ill, or as much notice as possible):

  • Message your mentor or another member of Student Faculty to inform them of your absence and reason
  • Submit an absence form (provided at the start of the year)
