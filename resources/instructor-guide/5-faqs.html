Instructor Guide 2026/27 — part 5 of 6.

How is information communicated with instructors?

Most information is delivered either by email in advance, or via a WhatsApp group chat. It's important to focus on teaching, but try to check messages during breaks for urgent updates.

What is the fail policy?

  • On exam night, students arrive 15 minutes before their exam slot for pre-exam practice. If they pass, they can go home.
  • If they don't pass their first exam, they get more practice followed by a second exam the same night. The second attempt is always examined by a member of senior faculty or student faculty.
  • If a student fails their second sit, they'll have a conversation with student faculty to arrange a second course — they must attend another two consecutive teaching sessions before a third sit is allowed.
  • In the unlikely event a student fails a third sit, we contact their course lead, who advises on next steps.

Should I use the manikin clicker?

In general, keep the clickers on your manikin off unless you're using them to check depth early on. They can be distracting during teaching, and are off during candidate exams.

How do I escalate concerns about a student?

If you're struggling with or worried about a student, escalate to your senior instructor or a student faculty member sooner rather than later. From there, we can arrange more focused teaching or help resolve the issue.

Do we have any socials this year?

Student Faculty organise a few socials throughout the year, paid for by the development fund. Attendance isn't compulsory, but they're good fun and a nice chance to get to know committee, senior instructors and assessors.

Is there a Canvas page for BLS?

All students sitting BLS this year should have access to the Canvas page with demo videos to revise in their own time — feel free to signpost this to them. If they don't have access, flag it to a member of Team Logistics.

I'm having trouble setting up my Worklink — what do I do?

All Worklink queries go to the Worklink team directly — student faculty doesn't have access to this system. Contact details are on the Admin Guide page.

I'm unable to attend a teaching session due to absence — what do I do?

  • Message your mentor / a member of student faculty as soon as possible
  • Fill in the absence form (see the Admin Guide page)
  • Student faculty may organise a return-to-work meeting following an absence, to make sure you're supported
