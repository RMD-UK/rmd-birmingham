Support & Development

Instructor Guide 2026/27 — part 4 of 6. References, mentors, reasonable adjustment plans, refreshments, and applying for future roles.

References

If you need a reference for something you're applying for, student faculty are happy to help — just ask beforehand. Your first port of call should be your mentor; if they're unavailable, contact the rest of student faculty and someone else will help. The content of the reference is at student faculty's discretion.

Mentors

At the start of the year you'll be assigned a member of student faculty as your mentor. They'll observe most of your teaching nights and send feedback to help you improve. Mentors are also your first port of call, alongside Senior Instructors, for any questions or problems.

RAPs (Reasonable Adjustment Plans)

RAPs are made for students who, for whatever reason (e.g. injury), can't perform the exam in the standard way. Student faculty decide whether a RAP is needed — it's your job to flag students who may need one:

  • First find out specifically what the problem is
  • Injury and compressions:
    • If injured but still able to perform compressions or the recovery position, no RAP is needed
    • If unable to do floor compressions due to injury, allow compressions on a table
    • If injury prevents any compressions or recovery position, instruct someone else to perform the skill
  • Alert a student faculty member about the student's problem
  • The student faculty member will decide whether a RAP is appropriate and discuss a plan with the student if necessary
Refreshments

We aim to provide small refreshments to enjoy during your break each teaching night — generally fizzy drinks and biscuit assortments. Please make sure any dietary requirements are known to Team Teachers on Student Faculty.

The student faculty table has its own separate refreshments/snacks, reserved for Student Faculty only. These are paid for out of student faculty members' own pockets to share amongst the team, not from the development fund — please respect that they're for Student Faculty only.

Applying for future roles

Near the end of Semester 2, applications open for future roles within the RMD BLS course. Student faculty are selected first, based on written application, observed teaching and interview. Once that's concluded, instructors can apply for Senior Instructor and BLS Assessor positions via written application. More information will be shared at the appropriate time — contact your mentor with any questions.
